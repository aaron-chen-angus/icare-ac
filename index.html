Arm Curl Biomechanics Lab

Setup protocol

  1. Straight-back chair without arms; sit tall, feet flat.
  2. Phone propped side-on to the dominant arm, 1.5–2.5 m away, around shoulder height.
  3. Weight in the dominant hand, arm hanging straight down (handshake grip).
  4. Keep the upper arm still; curl fully, turning the palm up, then lower fully.
  5. Do 30 seconds of curls after 1–2 practice reps.
